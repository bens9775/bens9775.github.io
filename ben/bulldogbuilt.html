My Bulldog Built Progress

During my undergrad years at Union University (2019-2023), the wellness center ran a fitness challenge they called Bulldog Built.

The goal of the program is to motivate students to achieve all aspects of fitness. There are fourteen tasks in ten categories from strength to endurance to flexibility. A student needed to meet a certain standard in every category to reach a certain level, whether bronze, silver, or gold.

For the first two years of college, I rarely exercised (beyond cycling as transportation and walking in the woods).
